Randombits now live on Github Pages

April 29, 2026

micro

Update: 2026-08-12: randombits.ca is no longer hosted on Github but rather on Cloudflare Pages

Despite the questionable quality of GitHub’s uptime and the recent incident with pull requests, I have decided to create a Github Pages mirror to increase the site’s availability, not that this site serves any grand purpose nor importance. More mirrors may be coming in the future, but this is something I have been debating ever since closing my previous blog, which has remained in archive-mode. Former visitors of that blog might be confused as to why the site has been completley changed. Hopefully, the merger of my Neocities microblog and my main blog has been seamless, such that visitors of both can visit any page with the original URLs without issue.

Here are the following mirrors as of the time of writing (August 12 2026):

  • Neocities: https://randombits.neocities.org/
  • Codeberg: zakuarbor.codeberg.page also accessible via randombits.ca
  • Github: https://zakuarbor.github.io/
  • Cloudflare: https://randombits.ca - Syncs with Github

The long-term goal is to rent a VPS to “self-host” the blog (at https://randombits.ca/) while maintaining the others as mirrors for readers who have their own platform preferences. Though neocities will remain my platform of choice for site discovery and community networking. Thus for the neocities community, nothing will change in the short and long term outlook of the site.
